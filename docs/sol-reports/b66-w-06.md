## Balance and behavior

The weapon is a deliberate medium-speed precision shot rather than a scatter piece: one projectile at speed 700, 0.015 spread, 600 behavior range, and a slow 0.75-second cadence. Its 12 direct damage is moderate; the first-hit branch is deliberately small at three jumps, 130 reach, 3 initial branch damage, and 0.7 falloff. A four-seed chamber and 1.8-second reload make target choice matter. The generated tier is 1 under the repository formula. Recoil is the sanctioned gun exception and is kept visually negligible at 0.0016.

The new family only aliases established systems: bolt-launcher firing audio and the existing long-gun pose language. It does not add or alter a rig, animation system, or subclass taxonomy.

## Painted ammunition and muzzle

The bespoke projectile is `thornhive-drill-seed`: a brown-black fat teardrop with exactly three raised spiral ribs, a pale hard tip facing right, and one acid-green rear leaf fin. Its installed manifest footprint is 192×73, with `mirror-upright` facing and a 58-pixel live display length. The first-hit three-splinter branch uses the gun mechanic's compact toxic branch VFX rather than generic scatter.

The muzzle is required and explicitly owner-assigned to the established `bloom` variant. It is tinted acid-green `#9CFF3B`, with a pale center and short thorn-petal read; `spark` timing keeps it compact and smoke-free. The art-derived mouth point is `(255, 55.5)` on the 256×112 sprite, so no hand-authored muzzle-coordinate override was needed.

The projectile faces right and preserves the three-rib/pale-tip/green-fin identity at gameplay size. Neither asset reads as a product photograph or clean vector icon. One accepted generation iteration was needed for each asset.
