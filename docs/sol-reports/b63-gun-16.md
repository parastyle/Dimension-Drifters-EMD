## Balance reasoning

The Helix is a compact, premium automatic rifle that trades peak impact for controllable sustained pressure. Its 7 damage at a 0.14-second cadence is 50 raw damage per second before reloads. The 26-round magazine holds 182 nominal damage and runs for 3.64 seconds under continuous fire; the 1.55-second reload leaves a meaningful punish window instead of making the compact layout a free advantage.

Projectile speed 1320, range 700, spread 0.025, and recoil 0.0009 make it precise and responsive at mid range without turning it into a long-range precision weapon. One point of pierce supports its next-issue identity and lets disciplined positioning matter, while DEX/STR scaling reflects both control and the physical cartridge mechanism.

## Art generation and visual check

I used the built-in image generator in reference-image mode, treating the two shipped rifles only as house-render references. It took two iterations:

1. The first established the correct compact rear-fed mechanism, continuous optic shell, and under-barrel combination unit, but its side planes were too soft and too evenly mid-grey.
2. The second preserved that geometry while forcing the values darker, flattening the cel planes, and strengthening both the silhouette outline and internal panel ink.

The final render went through the existing chroma-key, weapon slice, install, atlas, and muzzle-derivation path. The installed sprite is 256×95 RGBA with transparent corners. Its visible-pixel mean luminance is 26.9; 99.6% of visible pixels are below luminance 90 and 38.5% are below 24, placing its overall value at least as dark as the shipped references rather than in product-photography mid-grey.

Side-by-side inspection in `docs/sol-reports/b63-evidence/helix-bullpup-6-8.png` confirms:

- Muzzle and short exposed barrel face right; grip and deep rear magazine sit left.
- The magazine is unmistakably behind the trigger, so the weapon reads as a bullpup rifle.
- The optic housing is bonded into the raised carry rail as one continuous shell.
- Near-black charcoal dominates, with narrow cool highlights confined to top edges.
- The plain matched finish, even seams, and true assemblies read expensive and issue-grade, with no camo, salvage, tape, damage, or improvised repair.
- Heavy black ink defines both the outer silhouette and the internal shell panels, controls, magazine, seams, and housings.
- A single small muted-amber optic pane supplies the warm accent without emitting light.
- The rigid boxy under-barrel laser/light unit has a smoked front lens but no beam, glow, bloom, rays, or light cone.
- The proportions are chunky and game-readable, the mechanism remains credible, and the result does not read as a catalogue photograph.
