## Balance reasoning

Solaris is a tier-5 precision channel weapon. Four damage every 0.1 seconds becomes 40 raw DPS while the trigger is held, but the 12-pixel beam is deliberately narrow and asks for accurate tracking. Its 620 range makes the long focusing array meaningful without reaching beyond the beam system's established limit. A 0.85-second charge is slower than the older fast-start channel rifles; in return, 0.12-second sweep lag makes the optically matched beam responsive after it ignites.

The shared heat cycle limits a channel to 1.25 seconds, then imposes the standard overheat lock and cooling requirements. That prevents the high-focus output from becoming an uninterrupted damage hose. DEX/INT scaling represents steady aim plus energy-system control. The generated 24 px/s channel recoil remains subtle, and the closed manual tier floor preserves the owner-ordered flagship procurement identity. Its procedural beam recipe is a narrow solar-amber matched-lens column with converging strands.
