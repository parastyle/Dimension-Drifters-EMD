## Balance

The Micro-Lance is the roster's smallest beam gun: an agile close-range precision sidearm, not a full-length sustained channeler. Its 5 damage every 0.1 seconds produces 50 raw DPS after a 0.65-second charge. The exceptionally narrow 6-pixel channel rewards careful aim, while the short 360-unit reach forces the user into danger. A 0.05-second sweep lag makes the tiny emitter responsive without eliminating beam tracking entirely. Shared beam heat and overheat behavior prevent indefinite full-output use.

DEX/INT scaling reflects steady one-handed aim and emitter management. The 40-pixel display length is the allowed floor and establishes the smallest silhouette in the weapon roster. The deterministic budget is 17.153, placing it in Tier 2 between the 16.5 and 20 thresholds. That gives the compact sidearm a sharp specialist identity without matching the reach, forgiveness, or sustained control of larger beam weapons.
