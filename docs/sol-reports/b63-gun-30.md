## Balance reasoning

Aurora is a slow, brutal three-shot heavy ion gun rather than a rapid beam or generic rifle. Each packet deals 38 damage and pierces three targets, but its 0.82-second shot interval, three-packet core, and 2.8-second core reload make every discharge deliberate. The 800 projectile speed is visibly slower than a conventional high-velocity shot, while 760 range and 0.018 spread reward braced long-lane aim. A 2.6 visual scale makes the packet read as heavy energy without turning the weapon into an area-damage launcher.

Its deterministic tier budget is:

```text
38 damage × 0.85 projectile factor × 2.2 three-target pierce factor
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── × 1.1 long-range factor
                0.82 cadence + (2.8 reload ÷ 3 packets)

= 44.5814
```

That naturally clears the Tier 5 threshold of 44 without a manual floor. INT/STR scaling reflects core control plus the mass of an XL shoulder-fired chassis. The low capacity and long reload prevent the 38-damage packet from becoming sustained suppression, while pierce gives the cannon its own line-breaking identity.
