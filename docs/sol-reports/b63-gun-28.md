## Balance reasoning

Ember is a compact automatic plasma carbine rather than a beam, shotgun, or long-range precision weapon. Its 7-damage bolts fire every 0.16 seconds, but the 850 projectile speed is deliberately slower than the preceding issue-grade ballistic rifles. The 570-unit mid range and 0.025 spread reward controlled tracking without providing precision-rifle reach.

The 18-charge receiver bottle and 1.8-second reload create a visible energy-bottle rhythm and keep the high burst cadence from becoming uninterrupted. The deterministic power budget is:

```text
7 damage × 0.85 gun factor
────────────────────────
0.16 cadence + (1.8 reload ÷ 18 charges)

= 22.8846
```

The mid-range factor is 1.0, so the result lands in Tier 3 between the 20 and 26 thresholds. DEX/INT scaling represents target tracking and energy-system control. The generated physical recoil is a restrained 29 px/s, appropriate for a compact energy mechanism rather than a heavy ballistic shove.

## Art generation and visual check

I used built-in ImageGen with the two ordered shipped sprites as style-only references. The accepted art took **2 iterations**:

1. The first render established the correct one-gun mechanism, bottle, shroud, vents, gold furniture, optic, and attachment, but its smooth modeled highlights read too close to a polished product visualization.
2. The second render preserved the mechanism while darkening the broad planes, restricting highlights to top edges, strengthening exterior and internal ink, and simplifying the values toward the shipped dark-comic cel treatment.
