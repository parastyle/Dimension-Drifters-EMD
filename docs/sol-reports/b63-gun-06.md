## Balance reasoning

The Trailhead is a familiar, controllable patrol platform rather than a high-damage rifle. Its 7 damage, 0.24-second behavior fire rate, low 0.035 spread, 15-round straight magazine, and 1.45-second reload give it reliable close-to-midrange semi-automatic pressure without automatic-weapon volume. The 640 behavior range and 1120 projectile speed reward deliberate placement, while a single pierce and restrained recoil preserve the precise issue-carbine identity. DEX/STR scaling and two horizontal support points reinforce a compact, shoulder-fired two-hand platform.

## Visual check

Iteration 1 was rejected because its glossy lighting, broad magazine, and separate pistol grip read too much like a catalogue render. Iteration 2 corrected the form and house treatment and was accepted after inspecting the installed sprite beside both shipped references.

The final sprite:

- faces right and reads as one coherent semi-automatic pistol-calibre carbine;
- has a plain round barrel, squared receiver, straight-grip shoulder stock, hardwood forend, narrow belly stick magazine, and peep sight;
- is near-black with restricted top-edge highlights: mean opaque luma 29.2, with 94.7% of opaque pixels below luma 70;
- uses crisp, heavy silhouette and internal ink comparable to the references;
- reads as immaculate, precision-fitted issue hardware with no junk, repair, or salvage cues;
- uses amber-walnut hardwood as its single warm accent;
- carries a visible boxy under-barrel laser/light unit with a lens and no beam, glow, or light cone;
- remains flat-cel and visibly stylised rather than photographic.

The installed sprite is 256×79 pixels, contains no residual chroma-key pixels, and has transparent corners. The visual evidence copy is `docs/sol-reports/b63-evidence/trailhead-carbine-357.png`. Total image iterations: 2.
