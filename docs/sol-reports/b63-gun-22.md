## Balance and integration

Magnetar Coilgun is a slow automatic lane-puncher rather than another high-volume rifle. Each physical slug deals 15 damage, and the 0.52-second firing interval yields about 28.8 raw damage per second while the trigger remains held. Its seven-slug magazine stores 105 raw damage, but the 2.25-second reload creates a substantial sustained-output reset.

Projectile speed 2400, range 1000, spread 0.007, and three points of pierce define its identity: unusually fast, accurate shots that reward lining up a crowded lane. Modest 0.0012 recoil reflects controlled acceleration without pretending a physical projectile has no reaction force. DEX/INT scaling ties the weapon to precise handling and prototype-system expertise. Tier 4 marks it as advanced near-future issue hardware without placing it among the roster's most extreme tier-5 outliers.

The new `coil-accelerator` family is treated as a long gun for pose selection and uses the existing coil/rail mechanical report for firing audio. Its generated muzzle is `(255, 21.3)` on the rightmost barrel edge, derived directly from sprite alpha with no manual override.

## Art generation and visual check

I used the built-in image generator with the two owner-specified shipped sprites supplied as style-only references. The concept `artPrompt` above was the initial generation specification. The first render preserved the required mechanism but looked too much like a smooth product/catalogue illustration, so I rejected it. The second pass was a targeted style transfer that preserved geometry while replacing smooth modelling and thin technical lines with broad flat cel planes, near-black values, narrow top-edge highlights, and heavy exterior and interior ink. Total iterations: **2**.

The retained render went through the repository's existing flat-green chroma key, connected-component weapon slice, presize, post-key, install, sprite manifest, atlas, and muzzle-derivation path. It produced one connected 256×70 RGBA part. The four corner alpha values are all zero.

The required side-by-side evidence is `docs/sol-reports/b63-evidence/magnetar-coilgun.png`. Compared directly with the Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle references:

- One complete weapon faces right, with its battery and grip on the left and the open coil-ring muzzle on the right.
- The staged thick windings, open central spine, rigid top cable trunk, heavy flush rear battery, physical-slug magazine, and conventional trigger relationship read immediately as a ballistic coil accelerator.
- Near-black charcoal dominates: mean opaque-pixel luminance is 27.7, median is 23.2, the 75th percentile is 37.1, and 88.9% of opaque pixels have every RGB channel below 80. Highlights remain narrow and top-biased.
- The matched finish, aligned clamps, square battery seating, clean panel fit, and uniform windings read expensive, issue-grade, and well maintained. There is no tape, repair, salvage, mismatch, rust, dirt, or dangling hardware.
