## Balance

The weapon is a close-protection automatic, not a general-purpose rifle. Its identity comes from very low per-hit damage (4), a fast 0.075-second firing interval, short 500-unit reach, and 0.095 spread. The 20-round magazine gives roughly 1.5 seconds of continuous fire before a 1.15-second reload, so it can erase a nearby opening with about 53.3 raw burst DPS but falls to about 30.2 sustained DPS across full magazine/reload cycles. Projectile speed 960 keeps tight-space aim responsive; modest recoil and one-target pierce preserve disciplined issue-grade handling without granting long-range precision or crowd-clearing power.

The strict tier formula places it at tier 3. DEX supports its handling and cadence, while CON fits a close-protection platform that commits the user to short engagement distance.

## Art generation and visual check

Two image-generation iterations were required. Iteration 1 was rejected because it read too much like an evenly lit catalog rendering and did not clearly carry the ordered stock, foregrip, and magazine shapes. Iteration 2 was installed.

I rendered the installed 256×96 alpha sprite beside `x2-brimstone-gallows-rifle` and `x2-gravedog-auto-rifle` and visually confirmed:

- One complete weapon in flat side profile, facing right; the derived muzzle is at `(255, 28.5)`.
- The silhouette reads as a compact automatic personal defence weapon: stubby barrel, broad squared receiver, rigid folding wire stock, vertical foregrip, and short curved magazine.
- The base reads near-black charcoal, with restrained highlights on upper edges and deep lower planes.
- The finish is immaculate and issue-grade: fitted stock hinge, matched panels, true fasteners, and no tape, repairs, salvage, dangling pieces, or visual poverty.
- Heavy black ink defines both the outer silhouette and the receiver panels, seams, housing boundaries, fasteners, and furniture.
- Amber `#D6A648` appears as the single warm accent in the combo unit lens.
- Woodland olive/brown camouflage follows only the stock and furniture forms; the barrel, optic, receiver hardware, and combo unit remain bare dark metal.
- The small boxy laser/light combo is visibly mounted and contributes to the silhouette, with no beam, glow, cone, bloom, or spill.
- The result uses flat cel planes and chunky exaggeration and does not read as a product photograph.
