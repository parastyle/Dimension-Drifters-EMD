## Balance reasoning

Deacon Burstpiece is a controlled burst command sidearm rather than a full-volume spray weapon. Each trigger pull delivers three 4-damage projectiles at 0.075-second intervals, for 12 potential damage if the complete burst lands. The 0.32-second fire cadence, 0.05 spread, 520 range, and 960 projectile speed make it responsive at midrange without giving it rifle reach or perfect grouping.

The eight-burst magazine represents 24 projectiles and supports deliberate repeated engagements. Its 1.35-second reload creates a meaningful opening after that sustained output. Low recoil fits precisely manufactured issue hardware, while the burst commitment and moderate spread preserve the machine-pistol identity. The derived balance tier is 3.

## Art generation and visual check

The sprite used the built-in image-generation path followed by the repository chroma-key, slice, harvest, and install tooling. It took two image-generation iterations. Iteration 1 was rejected because its lighting and surface treatment read too much like a product render. Iteration 2 strengthened the near-black values, top-edge-only highlights, bold external and internal ink, flatter cel planes, and chunky prop proportions.

The final sprite faces right and reads immediately as one compact machine pistol: blocky slab slide, grip at left/below, long straight magazine, short right-side compensator, selector detail, and a small under-barrel box unit with a visible lens. The unit emits no beam, glow, or light cone. The finish is matched, clean, tight, expensive, and well-maintained rather than scavenged. Its base is near-black charcoal with restrained highlights on upper edges; opaque-pixel luminance has a 31.4 median and 42.3 75th percentile. Heavy black internal seams divide the slide, frame, grip, magazine, compensator, and accessory housing. Muted brass selector/control details provide the single warm accent.
