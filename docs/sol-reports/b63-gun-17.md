## Balance reasoning

Caseless Vanguard is a stable, high-volume automatic rather than a high-damage rifle. Its 0.1-second fire interval, 45-round magazine, low 0.035 spread, and restrained 0.00055 recoil sell the sealed prototype action and give it sustained mid-range pressure. Individual hits stay modest at 5 damage, with only one target of penetration, so its strength comes from accuracy and uptime rather than burst lethality. A 1.9-second reload creates a meaningful opening after a long string. The 1,350 projectile speed and 680 behavior range keep it responsive without competing with dedicated precision weapons. Tier 4 reflects exceptional issue-grade engineering while preserving room above it for the arsenal's most extreme weapons.

ArtKit chroma removal, one-component slicing, harvesting, atlas packing, and muzzle derivation produced the installed 256×69 RGBA sprite. The evidence image is byte-identical to that installed sprite.

I inspected the result beside both references. It is one connected weapon in full side profile, with the integrated stock and grip left and the stubby muzzle right. The broad flat top magazine, sealed rectangular body without an ejection opening, straight receiver tunnel, and short barrel make the caseless-carbine identity legible. The under-barrel box has a visible round lens but emits no beam, glow, or light cone.

The sprite's visible pixels have mean luma 37.3 and median luma 39.5; 98.7% are below luma 90. That places its darkness between the shipped references while retaining narrow top-edge highlights. Heavy black ink defines both the outer silhouette and the internal shell plates, seams, tunnel, grip planes, and attachment. The aligned panels, matched finish, and undamaged surfaces read as expensive and issue-grade rather than scavenged. Dark olive hex cells stay attached to the stock and lower furniture, leaving the tunnel, barrel, and laser/light unit bare. A small brass magazine latch provides the single warm accent. The chunky flat-colour rendering reads as a Dimension Drifters prop rather than a product photograph.
