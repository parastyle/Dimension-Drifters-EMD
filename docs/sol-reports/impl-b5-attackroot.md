# B5 — Weapon correctness: attack-authored root movement

## Initial implementation audit

Sparkknuckle Hex-Mitt (`x2-sparkknuckle-hex-mitt`) authors a `performance.forwardDrift` of 42 px/s for 0.34 s in `data/weapon-concepts-300.json`. Generation copies that datum into `packages/shared/src/weapons-expansion.generated.ts`. On every accepted main-hand swing, `GameRoom.resolveSwing` converts `forwardDrift` into a `PendingWeaponLunge` whose distance is `speedPxPerSecond * durationSeconds`; `GameRoom.stepPendingWeaponLunges` then validates an endpoint and rewrites the authoritative player position along that segment. The B5 fix is to delete only Sparkknuckle's `forwardDrift` source datum and regenerate, leaving its punch combo, glove-pair rendering, cadence, damage, and VFX data unchanged.

Thunderhead Stormfists (`x2-thunderhead-stormfists`) currently authors a 480 px, 0.05 s invulnerable lunge after the swing windup. `GameRoom.resolveSwing` currently registers the ordinary melee swing immediately, schedules quake/VFX from the ordinary swing clock, and independently schedules the root-motion lunge. Therefore damage/effects are not explicitly bound to the validated endpoint. The B5 implementation will give this attack an explicit destination-impact policy: capture aim on acceptance, validate and clamp the legal destination on the server at dash start, traverse the accepted segment in half the previous authored duration (0.025 s, twice the prior intended speed), preserve transit-only weapon-lunge iframes, and release the punch damage/quake/VFX exactly once after authority reaches that destination. The client rig will remain presentation-only and retain the existing attack animation.

## Implementation result

Sparkknuckle's `forwardDrift` was deleted from the source catalog and regenerated output. No combo, glove-pair, cooldown, damage, or VFX datum changed. `GameRoom` consequently creates no pending weapon lunge for any of its four combo beats. The focused server test proves an exact stationary start/end match across the full combo and an exact position/velocity match against an attack-free control while both move under ordinary locomotion. The SpriteRig presentation math is unchanged; the rig comment now states its shoulder/hip step is presentation-only, and the glove-pair test locks the approved four beat names, motions, hands, aura, and glove rendering.

Stormfists now authors `impactAtDestination: true` and a 0.025 s traversal for the existing 480 px lunge. `GameRoom` captures aim at acceptance, starts the dash after the authored windup, validates the endpoint once with the existing navigation/collision seam, traverses only that accepted legal segment, and keeps the existing one-server-tick weapon-lunge immunity. Its accepted melee swing remains collision-locked until arrival; release freezes the legal endpoint as the swing origin, detonates the quake once there, and prevents subsequent locomotion from moving the accepted hit. Damage and cadence remain 6 edge + 8 quake per 0.8 s, or 17.5 nominal DPS.

Stormfists presentation remains client-read-only. Its explicit recipe, caster-source paint, quake paint, and contact feedback delay until the destination can be authoritative and sample the synced player endpoint. The generic click-origin swing suite is suppressed for this explicit destination recipe, preventing a second origin impact. A focused ArenaScene test proves the caster cue waits through windup plus dash and reads the authoritative endpoint exactly once.
