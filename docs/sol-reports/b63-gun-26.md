## Balance reasoning

The Nova is a Tier 4 automatic mid-range energy repeater. Each pulse deals only 4 damage, but the 0.085-second cadence gives a 47.06 raw peak DPS stream. A full 36-pulse authored charge cycle lasts about 3.06 seconds; including its 1.6-second recharge, that is about 30.9 sustained authored DPS. This keeps the weapon's identity in disciplined continuous pressure rather than burst lethality.

Its 1,800 projectile speed, 700 range, and 0.018 spread make the packets responsive and accurate without reaching marksman precision. DEX/INT scaling represents tracking discipline and charge-system control. The low 0.00055 shot kick produces only 15 px/s of derived physical recoil, matching a rapid energy system rather than a heavy ballistic shove. The deterministic power budget is 26.266, which clears the Tier 4 threshold by a narrow margin and agrees with the authored Tier 4 assignment.

The new `pulse-rifle` family explicitly uses the long-gun pose and industrial-repeater fire-audio grammar. The boxy combination unit is recognized as both light and laser capability, while its sprite remains switched off with no baked emission.
