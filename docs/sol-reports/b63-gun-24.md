## Balance

The Prism Scatter Emitter is a deliberate close-range breacher rather than a rapid suppression gun. Each trigger pull produces six 5-damage energy shards for 30 raw point-blank damage. `pierce: 2` gives the precision splitter fan its identity: a well-aligned discharge can continue through one target, while `spread: 0.34` and `range: 420` keep that value dependent on close positioning. The 1,150 projectile speed makes the energy fan feel immediate without turning it into a beam.

Its five-shot charge cell, 0.58-second cadence, and 1.9-second reload create a measured fire/recharge rhythm. INT/STR scaling reflects emitter control and the physical handling of the thick front end. The deterministic tier budget is:

```text
(5 damage × 6 shards × 0.85 pellet factor × 1.6 pierce factor)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── × 0.9 close-range factor
             0.58 cadence + (1.9 reload ÷ 5 charges)

= 38.25
```

That places it in Tier 4, below the Tier 5 threshold of 44. It hits harder per accepted close fan than a conventional patrol gun, but the low charge count, broad spread, and deliberate cadence prevent it from becoming a sustained all-range answer.
