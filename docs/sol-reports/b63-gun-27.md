## Balance reasoning

Zenith is a deliberate, low-capacity Tier 4 precision weapon rather than a generic fast rifle. Its 15-damage pulse, 0.004 spread, 2,500-unit projectile speed, 1,050-unit behavior range, and two-target pierce reward exact long-range alignment. The 0.46-second shot interval, six-pulse lens cycle, and 2.1-second reload prevent that accuracy and reach from becoming automatic-rifle throughput. Negligible recoil fits a precisely trued photon emitter without removing the cadence and reload commitments.

The repository tier formula gives direct expected damage of `15 × 0.85 × 1.6 = 20.4`, amortized cadence of `0.46 + 2.1 / 6 = 0.81`, and a long-range factor of `1.1`. The resulting budget is `20.4 / 0.81 × 1.1 = 27.70`, which places the weapon inside Tier 4. Its raw no-pierce sustained rate is 18.52 damage per second and its six-shot raw magazine payload is 90.

## Art production and visual review

I inspected the shipped Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle sprites before writing the canonical prompt. I generated the sprite with the built-in ImageGen workflow in two iterations:

1. The first candidate established the very long open-frame silhouette, but I rejected it because its surface transitions were too smooth, the overall presentation leaned toward a product render, and the folded bipod did not read clearly enough.
2. The accepted correction retained the first candidate's geometry while using both shipped sprites as style references. The correction prompt required near-black flat cel planes, narrow top-edge highlights, much heavier outer and internal ink, a restrained amber-only lens accent, an immaculate matched finish, and two thin rigid bipod legs clipped rearward against the underside. It also preserved the strict right-facing profile, single connected gun, functional grip/trigger/sights/barrel relationship, and explicit absence of any rail-mounted light or aiming unit.

The canonical `artPrompt` printed in the concept entry above was the complete design prompt. The final accepted correction prompt was: “Restyle the first candidate to match the two shipped reference sprites: preserve its exact long, thin, right-facing photon-rifle geometry and open frame; replace smooth product-render shading with near-black hard cel planes, spot-black undersides, narrow cool top-edge highlights, bold outer contour, and equally heavy crisp internal panel ink. Keep the hardware immaculate and precisely aligned. Make the large forward lens and tiny optic witness ticks the only muted amber-gold accent. Make the folded bipod unmistakable as two thin rigid rearward-folded legs clipped flat beneath the frame. Exactly one connected gun on flat chroma green; no character, extra object, text, camo, dangling part, laser/light unit, beam, glow spill, gradient, or photorealism.”

The final installed sprite:

- faces right, with stock and grip left and the emitter muzzle at the rightmost edge;
- reads as a photon marksman rifle through its exceptionally long slender emitter barrel, oversized forward lens assembly, skeletal open frame, precision optic, and folded thin bipod;
- remains unmistakably a held gun through the shoulder stock, pistol grip, trigger guard, sight line, connected action, and straight barrel axis;
- has a near-black charcoal base, spot-black lower planes, and restrained highlights on top-facing edges;
- reads as expensive issue-grade hardware through its straight spars, concentric flawless lens housing, aligned optic, matched finish, and clean even seams;
- carries heavy black ink on both the silhouette and the internal frame, lens, optic, receiver, grip, and bipod divisions;
- uses muted amber-gold `#DCA33A` as the single warm accent;
- has no camo, laser/light unit, emitted beam, junk, repairs, dangling parts, text, hands, character, or background.
