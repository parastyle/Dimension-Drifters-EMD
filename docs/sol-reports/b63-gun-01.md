## Balance reasoning

This tier-1 familiar platform is fast, accurate, and deliberately weak. Three damage, a 0.12-second shot interval, low spread, negligible recoil, and a 10-round magazine make it comfortable for disciplined follow-up shots. The 1.15-second reload, single-projectile behavior, one-target pierce, and modest 610-unit behavior range keep it from competing with harder-hitting service rifles. DEX/LUK scaling reinforces precision and handling rather than brute force.

## Art production and visual review

I inspected the shipped Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle sprites before prompting. The first generated candidate was rejected because its wood was too evenly lit and realistic, its internal ink was too soft, and a projecting manual-action detail made the mechanism read incorrectly. The second candidate corrected the action to a flush semi-auto housing, deepened the charcoal and walnut values, limited highlights to top edges, strengthened all panel seams, and was accepted. Total iterations: 2.

The final canonical sprite:

- faces right in a strict, full side-profile;
- reads as a slim semi-auto rimfire with a long thin barrel, full hardwood stock, tiny sights, and a stubby belly magazine;
- uses a near-black metal base with restrained top-edge highlights;
- reads as immaculate issue-grade hardware through matched furniture, crisp checkering, and clean fitted seams;
- carries heavy exterior and internal black ink;
- uses warm honey-walnut `#B8874E` as its single accent;
- includes the small under-barrel laser/light box and visible lens with no beam, glow, or light cone;
- has no camo, dangling parts, text, logo, hands, character, or background.

Compared beside the two shipped references, it keeps their dark cel-painted, thick-ink prop language while retaining the intentionally lighter rimfire silhouette. It does not read as a catalogue photograph. The keyed PNG is 264×48 RGBA, has transparent corners, no residual key-green pixels, and one connected visible component at gameplay alpha. The evidence copy is byte-identical to the installed sprite (SHA-256 `31634141531A98F9252AB8169B96A8FC66E1B66FD0B48FEE0BF29D229ADB3FE2`).
