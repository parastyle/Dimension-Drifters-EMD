## Balance and implementation

Rimechoir is a direct-damage long-line caster, not a rapid bolt or an area detonator. A quick release deals 4 damage and a full 1.25-second charge deals 24; with the 0.85-second recovery, their idealized sustained rates are about 4.71 and 11.43 damage per second respectively. This makes an uncharged release deliberately inefficient while rewarding lane prediction and charge commitment. The projectile has no explosion damage.

Charge also grows the visible and authoritative capsule. Its radius scales from 9.9 to 27.9 pixels, while its half-length is 1.5 times its radius, producing the painted wedge's 2.5:1 full silhouette. Speed remains 620 and range remains 760 at every charge level. The player receives no movement, recoil, lunge, aura, or displacement.

The server gives this row its own `rimechoir-pressure-wedge` projectile kind and matching charged capsule. The client preloads and renders the bespoke painted sprite, uses the same aspect and scale for its charge preview, and preserves the projectile's right-facing authored orientation along the aim vector.

## Projectile and muzzle decision

The shipped projectile is a transparent painted object: three nested pale-cyan chevrons, black separating gaps, a white ice chip, and a ragged fractured front pointing right. It is neither a generic bolt nor a procedural translucent wave.

There is no ballistic muzzle, gun flash, smoke, shell, or gun recoil. The generated point at part 1 `(252, 42)` is an emission/focus origin at the rightmost fork only. On accepted release, the row's non-default frost/resonator caster recipe emits a brief cyan source flare at that point.

## Art generation and visual check

Generation used the built-in image-generation mode with the Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle sprites as style-only references. The final weapon prompt used the shape authority recorded in `artPrompt`; the projectile prompt requested one isolated right-facing, three-layer pale-cyan pressure wedge with black gaps, a small white core chip, a fractured leading edge, heavy ink, flat cel planes, and no weapon, character, text, ground, or glow field. The weapon took two iterations: the first was rejected for an overly vertical handle and product-like read. The projectile passed on its first iteration.

The final sprite and projectile were rendered with transparency and inspected both alone and in a contact sheet beside the two house references. The weapon faces right and reads as a caster/resonator: horizontal rear handle, narrow rigid frame, four pinned descending bars, bone-white longest bar, cyan repair sleeve, and a small right fork. It has a near-black base, cold top-edge highlights, heavy outer and internal ink, flat planes, and dirt/rust in the crevices. The cyan sleeve is the single dominant accent; sparse rusty-brown grime provides the warm wear note without becoming a second color block. No bar hangs or swings, and there are no hands, character, text, background, chains, tassels, or ropes.
