## Balance reasoning

Longwatch deals 12 damage every 0.36 seconds, or 33.3 nominal sustained damage per second before reloads. Its identity comes from 920 range, 1600 projectile speed, and two-target penetration: it hits harder and farther than an automatic service rifle, but its deliberate cadence and 16-round magazine keep it from replacing one at close range. Compared with slower manual precision weapons, its semi-automatic follow-up is quicker and its capacity is higher, while each shot is less brutal. The 1.8-second reload rewards controlled strings rather than indiscriminate fire.

## Art generation and visual check

I used the two shipped rifle sprites named in the order as visual references. The accepted generation used the concept `artPrompt` above, followed by one narrow correction: raise the muted dark-olive splinter patches by one flat value step and enlarge the same warm amber accents in the scope glass and combo-unit lens, while preserving the exact design, flat palette, heavy ink, direction, and lighting.

It took three iterations:

1. Rejected for reading too much like a product rendering and for showing an incompatible manual action detail.
2. Corrected the mechanism and flattened the rendering, but the camouflage and warm accent disappeared at shipped scale.
3. Raised only those two palette signals and accepted the result.

The final 256×61 sprite was rendered beside both references and inspected at native scale. It faces right and reads as a long marksman rifle through its slim precision barrel, slotted rail tube, raised cheek riser, and long scope. The base is near-black charcoal with restrained top-edge highlights. Bold exterior and internal ink define the housings, seams, rail slots, magazine, optic, and furniture. Its matched finish and true fittings look immaculate and issue-grade, with no tape, repairs, rust, salvage, or dangling parts.

The angular splinter camouflage remains attached to the stock and furniture surfaces; it does not float over the weapon. The barrel, optic housing, and laser/light housing remain bare dark metal. Warm amber is confined to the capped scope glass and the combo unit's visible lens. The combo unit is present as silhouette detail only, with no beam, glow, or light cone. The result uses flat cel planes and reads as a dark cartoon game prop rather than a catalogue photograph.

Automated alpha inspection found one connected component. Its opaque-core mean luminance is 33.3, closely matching the darker reference's 32.2, and 92.5% of its opaque core remains below luminance 80. The warm accent remains visible in 347 shipped-scale pixels.
