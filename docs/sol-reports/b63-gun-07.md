## Balance reasoning

Sidewalk Nine is a dependable semi-automatic service pistol, not a burst weapon or hand cannon. Six damage, a 0.19-second shot interval, 980 projectile speed, 560 range, 0.025 spread, and one pierce make it accurate and responsive without giving it rifle reach or crowd penetration. Its 17-round magazine and 1.2-second reload reward steady uptime. Low per-shot recoil and DEX-only scaling reinforce issue-grade consistency rather than exotic power.

## Art generation and visual check

Two art iterations were required.

1. The first pass had the correct one-gun silhouette, direction, inventory glyph, and combo unit, but broad soft shading made it too close to a polished product render. It was rejected.
2. The second pass repainted the same subject against the two shipped treatment references. After chroma keying, slicing, and canonical presizing, it retained hard dark planes, heavy exterior and internal ink, narrow top-edge highlights, and the single muted amber-brass glyph.

Final review beside the Brimstone Gallows-Rifle and Gravedog Auto-Rifle:

- Faces right; the derived muzzle is at the right edge, `(255, 21.6)`, with the grip on the left.
- Reads as one squared polymer pistol: slab slide, angled grip stippling, no external hammer, flat-faced trigger, short rail, and mechanically connected frame.
- Reads issue-grade and well maintained: matched finish, aligned panels, crisp stippling, no tape, salvage, mismatched parts, camo, or damage.
- Near-black treatment passes quantitatively: mean opaque luminance `26.14`; `95.3%` of opaque pixels are below luma 60.
- Heavy exterior and internal black linework remains legible at the installed `256x164` size.
- The muted amber-brass abstract inventory glyph remains visible as the sole warm accent.
- The boxy under-muzzle laser/light unit and its cool smoked lens are present only as silhouette details, with no beam, glow, cone, bloom, or spill.
- The RGBA sprite is one connected component with zero opaque keyable-green pixels.
