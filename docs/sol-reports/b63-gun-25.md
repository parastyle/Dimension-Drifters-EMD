## Balance

This is a quick duty sidearm with controlled short-range branching, not a heavy primary weapon. Its eight-damage main packet at a 0.32-second cadence yields 25 raw primary damage per second while firing. Ten charges carry 80 primary damage before the 1.45-second recharge. A fast 1,500-pixel projectile, moderate 520-pixel range, and 0.028 spread make it responsive without giving a small sidearm long-gun precision.

The identity mechanic is two short 150-pixel jumps for three damage each, with 0.72 falloff. At perfect occupancy the branches contribute 3 and 2.16 damage after the primary hit, but their short reach requires nearby targets. DEX/INT scaling expresses aimed sidearm handling and capacitor control.

The deterministic tier budget is:

```text
direct payload = 8 × 0.85 = 6.8
priced branch payload = (3 + 3 × 0.72) × 0.6 = 3.096
cadence = 0.32 + 1.45 ÷ 10 = 0.465
mid-range factor = 1.0

(6.8 + 3.096) ÷ 0.465 = 21.2817
```

That places the weapon in tier 3, above the 20-point threshold and below the 26-point tier-4 threshold. The generated catalog agrees: authored tier 3, derived tier 3.
