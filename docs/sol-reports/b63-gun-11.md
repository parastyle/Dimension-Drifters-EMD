## Balance

The Breachmaster is a sustained close-range pressure weapon rather than a single-shot burst shotgun. Each discharge launches seven low-damage pellets for 21 raw damage if the entire pattern connects. Its 0.24-second cadence and 20-round drum let it hold a doorway, while 0.28 spread and 380 projectile range make that output fall away quickly outside close quarters. The 2.6-second reload creates a meaningful vulnerability after a long automatic string.

The generated tier budget is approximately `43.42`:

```text
3 damage × 7 pellets × 0.85 pellet factor
───────────────────────────────────────── × 0.9 = 43.42
 0.24 cadence + (2.6 reload ÷ 20 shells)
```

That places it at the upper edge of Tier 4, immediately below the Tier 5 threshold of 44. This suits a frontline armoury flagship: unusually capable and expensive, but not a campaign-ending superweapon.

## Iterations and visual check

It took two generated iterations.

1. The first pass established the correct boxy automatic-shotgun mechanism, drum, carry handle, and accessory, but its even grey lighting read too much like a product render. I rejected it.
2. The second pass retained the mechanism and corrected the value structure, cel planes, and internal ink. I accepted and installed it.

Compared beside `x2-brimstone-gallows-rifle/part-1.png` and `x2-gravedog-auto-rifle/part-1.png`, the final sprite:

- faces right and reads immediately as an automatic shotgun through its enormous underslung drum, slab receiver, and precisely vented heavy shroud;
- is near-black overall, with narrow top-edge highlights rather than even mid-grey illumination;
- uses a heavy exterior contour and crisp internal ink around receiver panels, vent cuts, carry handle, drum rings, grip, stock, and accessory;
- reads as matched, precisely assembled, and professionally maintained, with no salvage, tape, improvised repair, dangling parts, or camo;
- uses one restrained warm brass accent at the machined drum hub;
- includes the small boxy laser/light unit and a visible unlit lens, with no beam, glow, or light cone; and
- reads as a chunky cel-painted game prop rather than a catalogue photograph.

The final PNG has transparent corners, zero opaque green pixels, one connected weapon component, and 97.87% of its opaque pixels below value 80; its mean opaque value is 29.54.
