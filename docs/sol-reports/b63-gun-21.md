## Balance and integration

Recoilless Whisper is a controlled covert automatic rather than a raw damage hose. Its 7 damage at a 0.14-second fire interval yields 50 raw sustained DPS before reloads. A 24-round magazine, 1.65-second reload, 0.018 spread, and 0.00065 recoil reward accurate tracking and disciplined bursts. The 640 range and 920 projectile speed keep it useful at mid range without giving it marksman-rifle reach, while one point of pierce gives its precise fire a modest crowd-control edge.

It is assigned prototype tier 4. The new family is classified as a long gun by pose selection and mapped to the restrained mechanical industrial-repeater sound set. Generator census pins and exact-count tests were advanced by one weapon only.

I used the shipped Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle sprites as style-only references, then generated through the existing image path, chroma-keyed, sliced, installed, and derived the muzzle point. - leaves the tube, optic, sights, and laser/light unit as bare dark metal;
- includes the required small boxy under-barrel laser/light unit with a visible unlit lens and no beam, glow, bloom, or light cone;
