## Balance reasoning

Precinct SMG-9 is a disciplined close-to-mid entry weapon, not a rifle substitute. Five damage at a 0.09-second cadence gives it a fast 55.6 raw damage per second before the shared Drive economy, while the 0.08 spread and 500-unit range constrain that output outside its intended envelope. A 30-round curved magazine supports a useful burst window, and the 1.35-second reload keeps its issue-grade handling brisk. Projectile speed 1080 makes entry shots feel immediate without reaching specialist long-gun velocity; one pierce and 0.0008 recoil keep the weapon controlled rather than brutal.

## Visual check against the shipped references

- **Facing and mechanism:** muzzle and short three-lug collar face right; grip and sliding wire stock face left. The trigger, grip, receiver, curved magazine, barrel and stock form a credible compact automatic weapon.
- **Submachine-gun read:** the slim tubular receiver, large rear sight drum, short muzzle, curved magazine and compact box attachment remain readable at runtime size.
- **Dark treatment:** opaque-pixel mean luminance is 30.5, versus 32.2 for Brimstone Gallows-Rifle and 47.6 for Gravedog Auto-Rifle. 98.0% of this sprite's opaque pixels remain below value 80. Highlights stay narrow and concentrated on upper edges.
- **Issue-grade finish:** all finish and hardware match; seams are tight and controls show deliberate polished contact points. There is no rust, dirt, tape, salvage, improvised repair or mismatched plate.
- **Ink and flat colour:** the silhouette has a heavy black boundary, with hard internal black divisions around receiver plates, welds, controls, sight, magazine and attachment. The render uses restrained flat dark planes rather than photographic shading.
- **Warm accent:** the single brass/amber accent is concentrated in the geometric inspection glyph and the attachment lens, clearly separating both details from the charcoal body.
- **Laser/light:** the boxy under-barrel unit and lens are visible as silhouette details. There is no beam, glow, bloom or light cone.
- **Subject isolation:** one gun only, no hands or character, no second weapon, no dangling elements, and no background remains after keying.
