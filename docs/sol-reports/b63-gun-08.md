## Balance reasoning

This is a familiar precision platform, not a rapid plinker or a giant anti-equipment weapon. Its 14-damage shot, 0.82-second cadence, five-round magazine, and two-second reload create deliberate but usable bolt-rifle pacing. The 960 authoritative range and 1,650 projectile speed reward long-lane accuracy, while pierce 2 gives a well-placed shot useful crowd value without turning it into a high-damage heavy rifle. DEX scaling, the long range band, controlled recoil, and deterministic Tier 2 assignment reinforce an accurate issue rifle that a large force can procure broadly.

## Sprite generation and visual check

The sprite took two ArtKit iterations.

- Iteration 1 was rejected because its clean, evenly resolved illustration read too much like a product-catalog image. The warm accent was also too small and the internal linework too soft.
- Iteration 2 strengthened the near-black value structure, restricted highlights to top edges, simplified the cel planes, made the internal black ink heavier, enlarged the amber bedding seam, and exaggerated the stock, barrel, rings, and bolt handle. This was accepted and installed through the existing chroma-key and weapon-harvest path.

I inspected the installed 256×66 sprite beside the shipped Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle references. It faces right and reads immediately as a scoped bolt rifle. The stock and furniture carry dark form-following digital blocks, while the barrel, action, bolt, scope, and rings remain bare dark metal. The finish is matched, tightly fitted, and maintained rather than scavenged. Heavy exterior and internal ink separate the stock panels, receiver, barrel flutes, tall rings, and optic. A warm amber bedding witness line is clearly visible at the action/stock seam. There is no laser/light unit, dangling element, text, character, or background.

The final sprite's nontransparent pixels measure 32.2 average luminance, 33.1 median luminance, and 60 at the 90th percentile; the shipped Brimstone reference measures approximately 30 median luminance. That confirms a near-black base with sparse upper highlights rather than a mid-grey overall read. Chroma inspection found no green spill. The derived right-facing muzzle point is `(255, 27)`.
