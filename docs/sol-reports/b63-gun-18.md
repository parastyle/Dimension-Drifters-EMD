## Balance reasoning

Rail-9 is a precision prototype sidearm rather than a spray weapon. Its 10 damage at a 0.38-second cadence gives 26.32 sustained pre-reload DPS. A nine-round magazine delivers 90 base damage before the 1.55-second reload, which rewards deliberate target selection without giving it rifle-like endurance. The narrow 0.018 spread, fast 1,800 projectile speed, 680 simulation range, and two-target penetration carry its conductor-assisted identity. Those advantages are paid for by the modest magazine, semi-automatic cadence, and small 10-damage hit; recoil remains controlled at 0.0014 because the institutional prototype is precisely built.

## Sprite generation and visual check

I used the shipped Brimstone Gallows Rifle and Gravedog Auto Rifle sprites as visual references in the image-generation workflow.

Iteration 1 was rejected because the rail treatment became too gold and the rendering leaned toward polished product art. Iteration 2 restored the dark house treatment but the two conductors merged visually at final sprite size. Iteration 3 separated both cool-steel rails with a black ink gap, kept amber only on the ceramic insulators, and was accepted.

The installed sprite is a 256×131 transparent PNG. Its SHA-256 is `F430D089429370292BD83B3466583CC3690B79D7470C08D22C73D73E1AFA16FA`; the evidence copy is byte-identical.
