# B66 row 03 — Hailbarrel Sledcaster

## Shipped result

Hailbarrel Sledcaster is one frostfell ranged weapon in the `puck-launcher` family. Its held art is a squat, right-facing cylindrical housing carried on one rigid curved lower runner. It fires a bespoke low ice round, applies a meaningful short slow, uses a compact bespoke icy muzzle flash, and has no ricochet or explosion.

## Balance reasoning

- The 13-damage round and 0.58-second cadence are moderate rather than burst-heavy. Including the eight-round magazine and 1.4-second reload gives about 17.22 sustained raw damage per second.
- Projectile speed is a fast, straight 1250 px/s over 620 px. Spread is zero, bounces are zero, and there is no explosion block.
- The hit applies `slow` at a 0.72 movement multiplier for 0.75 seconds: noticeable control without a long lockout.
- Physical recoil is explicitly limited to 64 px/s, with a small retained presentation kick of `0.0012`. This keeps recoil modest while respecting the sanctioned gun-recoil exception to the no-displacement law.
- The generated power budget is 16.6358, placing the weapon in authored tier 2. The control utility raises its value without turning it into a damage or area-clear outlier.

## Projectile, muzzle, and sound

- Bespoke projectile: `packages/client/public/projectiles/hailbarrel-sledcaster-ice-puck.png`, 192×100, rendered at display length 52. Its asymmetric contract is `mirror-upright`.
- The projectile is a pale-cyan flattened oval moving right, with two rear notches, three dark lower teeth, a tiny black lower runner, grime, and bold internal ink.
- Muzzle art: `packages/client/public/particles/hailbarrel-sledcaster-muzzle.png`, 192×96. The catalog decision remains semantic variant `shard`, while this weapon uses its own painted white center and exactly two cyan forward splinters.
- The flash is rooted at the alpha-derived bore point `(255, 26.5)` on the 256×80 weapon sprite. Three painted grey flecks plus two restrained runtime frost wisps provide the small frost-powder cue. Hailbarrel suppresses the generic brass casing.
- Fire audio resolves to the shipped mechanical `industrial-repeater` family, matching the scarred pressure-fused mechanism.

## Visual generation and check

Built-in image generation was used for all three bitmap subjects.

- Held prompt core: the exact shape-only `artPrompt` above, reinforced with the battered frostfell house treatment and a flat green key field. Iteration 1 was rejected because the lower support extended past the bore and the finish read too much like a dimensional product render. Iteration 2 was accepted.
- Projectile prompt core: one low pale-cyan flattened oval body traveling right, exactly two rear notches, exactly three dark triangular lower teeth, one tiny black lower runner, bold ink, grime, flat cel planes, and a green key field. Accepted on iteration 1.
- Muzzle prompt core: one compact right-traveling burst with a white diamond center at the left origin, exactly two pale-cyan angular forward splinters, a few small cool-grey powder flecks, rough flat-cel edges, and a green key field. Accepted on iteration 1.

The installed weapon and projectile were rendered on charcoal beside `x2-brimstone-gallows-rifle` and `x2-gravedog-auto-rifle`. Visual inspection confirmed:

- bore and projectile face right;
- the silhouette reads as a ranged cylindrical puck-launcher, not another row’s weapon;
- near-black casing, dark brown-black scarred planes, cold top-edge chips, crevice grime, heavy outer and internal ink;
- the uneven turquoise bore collar is the only bright held-weapon accent;
- the projectile’s two rear notches, three lower teeth, and small black runner remain readable;
- the result is neither a product photograph nor a clean vector icon;
- held and projectile PNGs have true alpha and zero remaining chroma-green pixels.
