### A1 why the old permanent gate passed

- The checked-in gate's final comparison is structurally tautological. `ArenaScene.syncProjectiles` puts a newly admitted container at `spawnAnchor`, then stores `spawnOriginX/Y` from that container and `spawnMuzzleX/Y` from the same `spawnAnchor`. `burst-origin-moving.spec.ts` compares only those two tags, so it proves that one assignment equals itself. It never reads/reconstructs the authoritative row origin and never samples the actual projectile container on the following frame, when `moveProjectiles` resumes dead reckoning and pulls it 18% toward the already-advanced authoritative `pr.x/y`.
- Coverage compounds the blind spot: the gate stops at exactly 12 rounds / three bursts, moves in one constant `+x` direction, never reverses, never crosses the magazine/reload/continued-fire interval, and reduces character movement to `distance(firstRig,lastRig) > 20`. It records no authoritative player position or velocity and places no bound on rendered/authority separation or per-frame character steps.
- Therefore the exact answer is: it checks presentation-vs-the-same-presentation assignment on only the admission frame, not authority-vs-render or the projectile's subsequent visible motion; it also samples too few one-direction bursts and does not grade the character at all. This is why its 0 px result coexisted with the baseline's 113.55 px authority/muzzle miss and 145.87 px character separation.
