## Balance

The Warden is a tier-4, two-handed frontline rifle that trades the volume and handling of a lighter automatic for disciplined hard-target bursts. Damage 9, 0.22-second fire rate, 20-round magazine, 760-unit projectile range, 0.035 spread, and pierce 2 make it forceful and accurate without turning it into a precision single-shot weapon. Its 1.9-second reload and stronger recoil preserve the cost of that authority. STR/DEX scaling and the long range band fit a heavy but controlled duty weapon. Its fire audio resolves through the existing heavy long-rifle family rather than the lighter automatic family.
