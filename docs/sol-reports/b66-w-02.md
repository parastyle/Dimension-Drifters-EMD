## Balance and mechanic

- Base cadence is medium at a 0.48-second cooldown and 15.0 nominal base DPS.
- The opening rake deals 4.464 damage at 0.92× range with only 4 knockback. Its 0.42× path arc keeps it deliberately light and narrow.
- The committed closing pinch deals 11.376 damage at 1.08× range with 10 knockback. Its 0.18× centre-line capsule rewards exact alignment instead of broad coverage.
- `comboForwardPx: 0` makes both beats planted, satisfying the no-character-displacement law.
- The existing `pivot` swing style describes the hinged action and intentionally resolves the authored `rake` combo through the existing close-blade pose family without adding a new rig or taxonomy.
- The deterministic power-budget derivation assigns tier 1; no manual tier floor was introduced.

The built-in image generator was used. The final weapon direction is preserved verbatim in the `artPrompt` above. The final VFX direction was: “Isolated melee hit VFX only on a flat solid #00ff00 field: two separate slim cold-cyan cel-painted jaw arcs start far apart at LEFT, curve inward, and converge at one strike centre on the RIGHT; at convergence place one small sharp white diamond-shaped ice fracture and exactly four tiny near-black chips. Heavy irregular ink edges, chipped painted frost planes, no weapon, character, hands, projectile, text, aura, smoke, background scene, gradient, or clean vector treatment.”

The weapon took two image-generation iterations plus one alpha-edge matte correction. The VFX took two image-generation iterations; the second reduced the arc thickness and length to retain the precise closing-jaw read.

Final visual audit against `x2-brimstone-gallows-rifle/part-1.png` and `x2-gravedog-auto-rifle/part-1.png`:

- Faces right and reads immediately as one two-arm melee pincer, with both closed hand loops on the left.

- The cold-cyan pivot is the signature accent; the single chipped bone-white upper jaw supplies the warmer counteraccent without making the silhouette clean or issue-grade.

- The final alpha assets contain no remaining `#00ff00` key pixels and have transparent corners.
