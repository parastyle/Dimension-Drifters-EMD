## Balance reasoning

The Cyclone Micro-Gat is a compact suppression tool rather than a precision or burst-damage weapon. Its 3 damage at a 0.065-second fire interval yields roughly 46.15 raw sustained DPS while firing. A 48-round box supports 3.12 seconds of uninterrupted fire, then imposes a 1.75-second reload. The fast 960 projectile speed and 570 range keep the torrent responsive, while 0.10 spread, modest damage per hit, and no additional piercing prevent that fire rate from becoming a precision boss melter.

DEX/STR scaling reflects the combination of feed control and physical handling. The existing deterministic tier formula places this profile at tier 3. The rotary-heavy audio assignment gives the mechanism its own audible identity without changing its ballistic behavior.

## Sprite generation and visual review

I generated against both shipped references named in the order. Iteration 1 established the correct six-barrel mechanism and furniture layout but was rejected because its polished lighting still read too much like a catalogue render. Iteration 2 retained the mechanism while forcing the values darker, the colour planes flatter, and both exterior and interior ink substantially heavier. The second render was accepted, then chroma-keyed, sliced, and installed through the existing gun-art path as one 256×106 RGBA sprite.

Visual comparison against both references confirmed:

- Muzzle faces right; rear grip and stock remain left.
- The short six-barrel cluster, sealed drum drive, top box magazine, vertical foregrip, and connected grip-to-barrel mechanism read immediately as a compact rotary weapon.
- Near-black charcoal dominates, with sparse highlights confined to top-facing edges.
- Housing seams, barrel divisions, panels, and the outer silhouette use crisp heavy black ink.
- Construction reads immaculate and expensive: aligned components, matched finish, no repairs, tape, salvage, rust, or dangling parts.
- The brass feed throat supplies the single selected warm accent.
- Red tiger paint follows only the stock and grip furniture forms; barrels, optic, magazine hardware, and laser/light unit remain bare dark metal.
- The boxy laser/light unit has a visible dark lens but emits no beam, glow, cone, or bloom.
- The sprite contains one weapon only, with no character, text, logo, background, loose ammunition, or second object.

Pixel inspection found 12,443 visible pixels, zero green residue, transparent corners, a mean visible-pixel luminance of 19.8, 663 red-camo pixels, and 82 brass-accent pixels.
