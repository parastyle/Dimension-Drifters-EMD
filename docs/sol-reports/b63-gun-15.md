## Balance reasoning

The weapon is deliberately the opposite of a light plinker: 40 damage, six-target penetration, 2,400 projectile speed, and 1,100 behavior range make each hit feel like a long-range armour-breaking event. That output is constrained by a two-round magazine, a 0.9-second firing cadence, and a three-second reload. Its 224-pixel display length, XL class, two-handed grip, bolt handling tag, and dedicated bolt-hand point carry the same weight into presentation and pose. Tier 5 reflects top-line frontline issue quality.

## Generation and visual check

I used the built-in image generation path once, with `x2-brimstone-gallows-rifle/part-1.png` and `x2-gravedog-auto-rifle/part-1.png` supplied only as house-style references. The generation prompt followed the concept `artPrompt`: shape description rather than any real-world designation, near-black cel treatment, heavy external and internal ink, form-following desert tan on stock and furniture, bare barrel and optic, and a restrained brass latch accent. ArtKit chroma removal and weapon harvesting produced one 448×80 RGBA sprite.

The installed sprite and evidence copy were inspected beside both references. The muzzle points right and the stock/grip left. The long fluted barrel, oversized arrow-shaped brake, cut-out chassis, thick pad, folded bipod, huge capped scope, and protected bore read immediately as an anti-materiel rifle. Its visible-pixel mean luma is 46.6, essentially matching the dark Gravedog reference at 46.1; 83.2% of visible pixels are below luma 90. Top-edge highlights do not flatten the near-black body. Outer silhouette and panel seams are strongly inked. The matched finish is clean, uniform, and expensive rather than scavenged. Desert tan stays attached to stock and fore-end forms while barrel and optic remain bare; brass cap latches provide the single warm accent. It reads as a stylised Dimension Drifters prop, not a product photograph. No laser/light unit, hanging object, text, character, background, or second weapon is present.
