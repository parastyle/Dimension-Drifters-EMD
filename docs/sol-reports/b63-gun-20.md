## Balance reasoning

The Smartlink Burstmaster is an accurate command rifle built around committed three-round bursts rather than continuous volume. Each projectile deals 6 damage, so a complete burst delivers 18 raw damage. The 0.065-second intra-burst spacing completes the three shots in 0.13 seconds, while the 0.42-second firing cadence limits repeated bursts to about 42.9 raw DPS before Drive, economy, and target mitigation.

The ten-burst magazine represents 30 physical rounds and 180 potential raw damage per load. A 1.7-second reload gives the sustained output a real reset. Projectile speed 1450, range 850, spread 0.012, and recoil 0.0008 express the sensor-assisted precision identity without adding impossible steering or energy ammunition. One pierce keeps it a disciplined single-lane rifle rather than a crowd-clearing heavy weapon. DEX/INT scaling reflects accurate handling plus smart-system use, and the existing long-rifle fire-sound family preserves its ballistic character.
