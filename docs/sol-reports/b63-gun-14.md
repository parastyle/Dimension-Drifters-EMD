## Balance reasoning

The weapon is a compact, fast specialist gun rather than a weak spray tool. Six damage at a 0.10-second cadence gives 60 raw burst DPS before Drive and economy modifiers. A 25-round magazine carries 150 raw damage per load, while the 1.5-second reload creates a clear reset between close-range bursts.

Projectile speed 880 and range 470 keep it below rifle reach. Spread 0.07 and recoil 0.0011 preserve controllability without making the short platform laser-flat. One pierce rewards disciplined lanes but does not turn it into a crowd-clearing rifle.
