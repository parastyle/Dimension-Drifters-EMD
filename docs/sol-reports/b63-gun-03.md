## Balance reasoning

This is the dependable middle of the patrol-rifle spectrum, not a bullet hose or a specialist marksman weapon. Seven damage, a 0.22-second authored firing interval, 0.025 spread, and 1,200 projectile speed make deliberate semi-automatic follow-up shots feel quick and precise. A full 30-round magazine and 1.55-second reload support issue-grade service use, while the manual cadence, restrained recoil, and single-projectile behavior preserve its identity against automatic rifles and heavy long guns. The derived progression tier is 3.
