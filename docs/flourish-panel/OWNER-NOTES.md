# Owner amendments — flourish system (binding, 2026-07-18)

Read this ALONGSIDE vision.md/spec.md; where they conflict, this wins.

1. **Flourishes are FULL-BODY performances, not weapon-sprite spins.** When it is
   tasteful, the body participates: lean into a sword flip, a small hop or foot
   shuffle on a catch, shoulders (body card tilt) selling the twirl. The weapon
   alone rotating over a statue is exactly the failure mode to avoid. Tasteful =
   small, readable, silhouette-first — the pose-panel principles apply.

2. **The head is on the jiggle roster.** The floating head is a first-class sprung
   part (commit daf40b4) and, when the wardrobe replacement system lands, heads are
   baked/replaceable (helmets, masks). Flourish design must include head beats:
   the head TRACKS a circle-flip (small follow arc), tilts on a stow, dips on the
   draw's anticipation. Everything routed through the existing head spring channel —
   never a separate animation path — so helmets/masks and the prestige tower ride
   along automatically.

3. Reduced-motion still gets the SETTLE (weapon returns home, body relaxes) with
   rotation and hop removed.
