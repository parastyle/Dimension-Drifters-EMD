## The panel's questions (address each)
1. **When are hands "dead"?** Taxonomize the states — one-handed weapon (off-hand idle), thrown weapon
   between throws (both hands recover), reloading gun (support hand pumps), charging/channeling caster
   (off-hand does nothing), during a swing's recovery frames, etc.
2. **The wrong-side bug** — WHY does the off-hand end up on the far side of the facing direction for
   many 1H weapons? (Trace it in code — is the off-hand pinned to a rig-local x that isn't mirrored on
   facing? Or is it default-parked at world "left" regardless of facing?)
3. **Same for feet** — do idle feet drift under the character or land in weird gaps during runs or
   swings?
4. **Design language for "alive"** — what SHOULD the idle-side hand do? Options (rank them and pick a
   default per weapon class):
   - Rest on the hip / belt (character-side).
   - Hover at low guard on the facing side (ready but not committed).
   - Mirror-guard (extended toward the target, defensive posture — think fighting games' off-hand).
   - Rest on the equipped weapon secondary point when the weapon supports it.
   - Fingers curl / passive vs interactive (secondary weapon flourish / prop).
5. **The systemic fix shape** — propose a compact rule set the impl Sol can execute:
   - A per-weapon-class default "idle-hand pose" (a small vocabulary — 4-5 poses tops).
   - A universal mirror rule: idle appendages ALWAYS resolve to the FACING side, never the far side.
   - Data hooks: a `poseLanguage.idle` datum per weapon class (or per weapon override) with the pose
     name; the SpriteRig applies it. What columns/fields would you add to the data + generator?
   - Testability: how do you unit-test "no idle appendage on the far side across every weapon"?
6. **Interaction with existing systems**: dual-wield (both hands active — the idle rule doesn't apply),
   thrown recovery (both hands recover on facing side), casters channeling (off-hand doing the
   "casting" gesture, not idle), guns (off-hand as foregrip already handled).
7. **Feet during idle vs during action** — should feet always resolve to a "planted natural stance"
   regardless of upper-body pose? Any weapon-class specific stance (samurai wide stance vs pistol
   loose stance)?
