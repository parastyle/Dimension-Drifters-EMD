   Bosses/elites ignore or have their own token budget (document your choice).
3. **Two-channel telegraph.**
   - WIND-UP: gradient full-body tint ramp from the enemy's base look toward its ACCENT color
     (per-enemy-kind accent from its palette; e.g. brown wolf with red eyes ramps red). Ramp
     DURATION varies per enemy kind (fast mobs short, heavies long) — this is enemy personality.
   - COMMIT: one crisp universal WHITE POP frame (plus a tiny anticipation crouch/squash) at the
     end of the ramp. Identical presentation across all enemy kinds. Colorblind-safe by design
     (white pop + squash are hue-independent). Pair with a short per-kind commit SFX from the
     shipped sample families.
4. **Fixed commit-to-impact window, locked lunge.** From white pop to damage is a CONSTANT
   ~200ms across ALL melee enemies (single shared constant; this IS the parry-timing window).
   The lunge vector LOCKS at the pop (modest tracking allowed only during wind-up). During the
   locked lunge the enemy travels its lunge distance server-authoritatively (nav-validated).
   The hit lands unless the player is in dodge-roll i-frames, parries it, or is displaced beyond
   the locked vector's reach by their own verb (jump counts). Walking/strafing does NOT evade.
5. **Player attack-slow rule.** Verify how player movement interacts with attacking: the rule is
