## Why this exists — read the precedent first
Read `docs/sol-reports/order-b63-modern-guns.md` in full, especially amendments A1–A5. That order is
the working template and it was learned the hard way:

- **A1 — NEVER NAME A REAL WEAPON in any prompt, theme or artPrompt.** Naming a real model makes the
  image generator retrieve its catalogue photo and render it realistically, which overpowers every
  style instruction. An entire wave was discarded for this. Describe the SHAPE, never the NAME.
- **A5 — DIMENSION = WEAPON CULTURE.** The B63 guns are the player force's own issue-grade kit. The
  battered, improvised, fantasy hardware already shipped (coffin magazines, gallows rifles, wrapped
  grips) belongs to OTHER dimensions and is explicitly still wanted.
- **ONE SOL, ONE WEAPON.** Never batch subjects; themes bleed.

**These 20 are NOT more cop-dimension guns.** B63 already covers that. Make these the *other*
dimensions' weapons — the trashy, fantasy, characterful hardware the owner said they want to keep
making. Draw variety across melee, caster and ranged rather than 20 more rifles.

## What each of the 20 rows must specify
1. **Name** in the house voice (look at shipped names: Brimstone Gallows Rifle, Dustdevil Riotgun,
   Quicksilver Streetstomper, Driftblade).
2. **Family / type** (`melee` | `ranged` | `caster`) and where it sits against the existing roster —
   every row needs a reason to exist that is not "another sword".
3. **FORM: a pure shape description containing NO real-world proper noun.** This is what reaches the
   prompt. Concrete and drawable.
4. **CHARACTER: the fantasy/gamification hook** — what makes it a Dimension Drifters prop.
5. **Its dimension / culture** — which world's hardware is this? Use the shipped dimension themes
   where they fit (`frostfell`, `verdant-ruins`, `ashlands`, `neon-cyber`, `wild-west`).
6. **PAINTED AMMUNITION — mandatory, owner-ordered.** Every one of the 20 gets its OWN bespoke
   projectile art. Specify what the projectile IS. Look at
   `packages/client/public/projectiles/` for the established treatment. A melee weapon with no
   projectile still needs its signature hit/arc VFX specified instead — nothing ships generic.
7. **Muzzle flash** where the weapon has a muzzle — see `tools/artkit/gen-weapon-muzzles.mjs` and
   `packages/client/public/muzzle-reference/` for the existing language. Say when one is NOT needed.
8. **Balance intent** in one line, so 20 Sols do not all pick identical numbers.
