## Root cause is already measured — do not re-derive it

The Lava Foundry walkable polygon is FAR smaller than the drawn platform. Measured walkable share of
the visible silhouette:

    broken-glass-observatory        30.8%
    broken-turntable-arena          34.5%
    broken-lavafall-overlook        35.5%
    broken-reactor-arena            36.5%
    broken-security-gate-platform   45.0%

The trace itself is GOOD — `derived-alpha-v1`, alpha threshold 40, 12px cell, 64-263 polygon points
per platform, holes cut out. The floor is lost AFTER tracing, to two settings in the collision
provenance:

    edgeInsetPx: 48            // walkable edge pulled 48px inside the silhouette, every side
    bottomTrimFraction: 0.13   // bottom 13% of the silhouette discarded outright

So the simulation believes the player is over lava while they are standing on plainly drawn floor.
That is bug 2 (hurt before the edge) and bug 3 (hurt mid-gap, then knocked back by the pit impulse).
Bug 1 is very likely the same cause: a jump that starts from inside-the-art-but-outside-the-polygon
fails its ground test and resolves immediately, which reads as a ~0.1s hop. `JUMP_AIRTIME` is 0.55s
and `DIST_JUMP_AIRTIME` is 0.6s, so 0.1s is an abort, not a tuning value.

VERIFY that chain before fixing. If bug 1 turns out to be independent of the polygon, follow the
evidence and say so.

## Fix

1. **Re-derive collision with a defensible inset.** 48px is arbitrary. The only physical reason a body
   cannot stand at the very edge is its own radius, `PLAYER_RADIUS` = 24. Prefer inset 0 in the stored
   polygon and apply the radius at TEST time (so the same art serves any future body size); if you keep
   a baked inset, justify the number.
2. **Justify or delete `bottomTrimFraction`.** If it exists to stop actors standing on a painted
   under-hang that reads as wall rather than floor, keep it and say which platforms need it and why.
   If it is a blanket 13% haircut, delete it.
3. **Fix the jump abort** so a jump always runs its authored airtime unless something real interrupts
   it (landing on geometry, damage, parry, pit). A jump must never be cancelled by the takeoff tile
   test alone.
4. Re-measure walkable share after the change and report it per platform. Target is the drawn floor
   being walkable — expect ~80-90%, not 100% (holes and real edges are legitimate).
