## Deliverable 1 — GUARANTEE the two invariants, by construction

**INV-A (separation):** no two platforms' WALKABLE COLLISION SURFACES may touch or overlap. Minimum
clearance ≥ a named constant (start at the existing 72px target floor).
**INV-B (traversability):** every graph edge's crossing must be ≤ the shipped distance-jump budget,
keeping the existing margin under `DIST_JUMP_REACH`.

Requirements:
- Place BY CONSTRUCTION along the connection graph, not by guess-and-reject. For each edge, choose a
  direction and a separation sampled from `[MIN_CLEARANCE, MAX_JUMP_BUDGET]` measured between the two
  platforms' actual surfaces, then position the child accordingly. Construction that satisfies the
  invariant by arithmetic cannot "fail".
- **Measure clearance on the COLLISION SURFACES, not the image rect and not `visibleBounds`.** These
  PNGs carry transparent margins (e.g. a 1368x1007 image with visibleBounds `[32,32,1304,943]`), and
  the platforms are irregular. Rect-vs-rect is the wrong metric in both directions: it reports
  overlap where only transparency meets, and it reports clearance where two silhouettes actually
  touch. `collisionBounds` / the collision polygons are the truth. Say what metric you used.
- **Delete the 32-attempt throw.** If a construction genuinely cannot place a room (e.g. a 4K hero in
  a tight arena), it must DEGRADE deterministically — pick a smaller prefab, reroute, or shrink the
  graph — never throw and never ship a touching pair. State the degradation ladder.
- Keep generation DETERMINISTIC and seeded. Same seed must produce the same map.
- Respect `MAP_MARGIN` and the arena bounds as now.
- Keep `nativeScale: 1`. Platforms stay at natural render; do NOT solve crowding by scaling art down.

Keep a final verification pass that asserts INV-A and INV-B over the produced layout. With correct
construction it can never fire — that is the point. It is a tripwire, not the mechanism.
