## Fix
Entering a distance jump must be a clean handover from `MOVE_SPEED` locomotion to `DIST_JUMP_SPEED`
travel with NO injected displacement:
- No frame may render more travel than the active speed allows for that frame's delta. The authored
  speed changes at the transition; the DISTANCE covered in a frame must still equal speed x dt.
- The frame-sampled preview lead must not collapse and rebuild at the transition. If the jump commit
  legitimately consumes the pending window, the render path must account for what it consumed rather
  than double-count it — that double-count is the most likely source of the ~60px.
- Do NOT fix this by easing or ramping the speed change (canon L09: one constant speed per state, feel
  comes from animation). The transition is instantaneous; what must not happen is EXTRA distance.
