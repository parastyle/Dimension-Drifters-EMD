## Part 1 — THE LAW: one constant speed
**The character moves at ONE CONSTANT SPEED.** The only permitted modifiers are:
- attacking
- parrying
- an explicit environmental slow (ice or similar)

Nothing else may alter speed. No lurching, no pulsing, no per-direction variation, and specifically
**no diagonal anomaly**. Diagonal movement must be the same speed as cardinal movement — if the
vector is not normalised somewhere, or is normalised twice, that alone can produce exactly the
"consistent lumpy lurches" being reported.

The report says **diagonal BACKWARDS-DOWN** specifically, and calls the lurching *consistent* —
periodic, not random. That is a strong hint: look for something phase-locked. Candidates worth
checking early — a locomotion/gait cycle feeding back into velocity, the turn-hitch system
(`MOVE_HITCH_MIN_ANGLE`, `MOVE_HITCH_DIP`, `MOVE_HITCH_MIN_SPEED` in
`packages/shared/src/constants.ts`) mis-triggering when facing and movement disagree, a
backpedal/reverse modifier, or depth-sort/Y-band interaction on downward motion.

Facing away from the movement direction is the distinguishing feature of "backwards" — check any code
that treats move-vector-vs-aim-vector divergence as a reason to modify speed.

**Add a regression test**: sample speed over a run of frames in all 8 directions, forward and
backpedalling, and assert it is CONSTANT within a tight tolerance. That test is the deliverable that
stops this recurring.
