## 1am summary

Keep **32 of the 40 playable identities**, led by the Drifter as the shadow-faced north star, and cut Cordell, Buzzard, Dunkel, Deepfall, Halcyon-7, Grix, Pyra, and Sir Galloway because their core reads are delivered more strongly by retained characters. ## Roster decision: 32 characters

Carry **32 identities** forward: the Drifter plus 31 legacy `cc-*` characters. This preserves 80% of the playable roster and all of its strongest identity families, while cutting eight characters whose central read is already delivered more clearly elsewhere. Thirty-two is still generous enough to honor “our old characters before wardrobe,” divides cleanly into practical batches of eight, and reduces whole-character authoring from 80 bespoke body/head deliverables to 64 before revisions or animation QA. The ordering below is an **art-production priority ranking**, not a power tier list.

The Drifter is the test every other identity must pass: at gameplay size, is the character as immediate as “shadow-faced road ghost in a battered hat and duster,” without needing a visible expression, prop inventory, or lore paragraph? A retained character does not need the Drifter's clothes, but does need an equally decisive head mass, torso mass, and one signature.

## Concrete production plan for this track

1. Freeze the 32 IDs and the exact names in the ranked bible as the canonical identity input. Do not let prompt iteration rename, merge, or add characters.
2. Approve the Drifter alone first. The accepted Drifter fixes the black-face value, no-neck overlap, body/head proportion, outline weight, material rendering, and gameplay-size readability for every later comparison.
3. Author in four review blocks, using rank order: **anchor block** ranks 1–8; **occult/material block** ranks 9–16; **western/duelist block** ranks 17–24; **motion/support block** ranks 25–32. A block does not advance until every head passes face law and every silhouette remains distinct in an eight-character monochrome lineup.
4. For each ID, preserve exactly three canonical identity fields from this report in the prompt data: one-line fantasy, silhouette read, and one visual signature. Palette and art-brief details from `character-concepts.json` support those fields but may not overrule them. The body/head identity must still read empty-handed: a listed weapon, held prop, or transient spell effect is fantasy evidence, not permission to bake it into the character rig or make recognition depend on it.
5. Review identity in this order: black silhouette at gameplay size; head-only silhouette at 0.85 mount scale; face-law close-up; body/head overlap through bob extremes; palette/material pass; finally four-player lineup. A pretty portrait that fails an earlier gate is rejected.
6. Keep every retained spread and quirk ID unchanged during art migration. Keep cut-quirk handling explicit for migration: `coldsnap`, `overstuffed-bandoliers`, `hazard-rates`, `mag-boots`, `half-projection`, `braced`, `let-it-out`, and active `the-unbending` become orphan candidates; do not silently transfer any of them to a retained identity.

## Cost and dependencies on the other four tracks

The minimum roster payload is **32 canonical prompt records, 32 promoted whole-character identities, 32 extracted character-owned heads, 32 corresponding bodies, and 32 gameplay-size/monochrome/bob QA rows**.
