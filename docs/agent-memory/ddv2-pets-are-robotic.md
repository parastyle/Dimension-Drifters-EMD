DDv2 pets are ROBOTIC/MECHANICAL — drones, constructs, chrome, servos, plating, glowing optics,
antennae, jointed limbs, exhaust, little mechanical critters. NOT organic.

**Why:** the owner saw a pet that looked like a "rock-turd" (an unappealing organic lump) and rejected
it. **Anti-example to design away from:** shapeless organic blobs, rock/stone lumps, mud/turd shapes,
amorphous ooze.

**How to apply:** the pet roster's organic concepts are recast as robotic equivalents; the pet art
generation pipeline gets an explicit review gate that REJECTS any render reading as an organic lump.
Robotic ≠ cold/ugly — cute robots, majestic drones, weird contraptions all qualify; the through-line
is "built, not grown." Recorded in docs/design/OWNER-DECISIONS-characters-pets.md item 7. Relates to
[[ddv2-art-gen-one-subject-per-agent]] (one subject per agent still applies to pet art).
