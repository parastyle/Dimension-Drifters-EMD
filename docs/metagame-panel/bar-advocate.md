# Drive resource — B27 invariant review

B27 narrows the weapon topology without changing the shared resource economy.

1. Drive value and recovery debt are player-global.
2. Weapon cadence debt belongs to the exact selected instance and continues aging while stored.
3. Selecting another Active position never initializes or refills Drive.
4. Only the selected weapon definition can request an accepted attack or debit.
5. A pre-made dual is one catalog definition and follows one debit path.
6. No second equipped instance, secondary magazine, secondary charges, secondary cooldown, damage
   contribution multiplier, or throughput cap survives.
7. Beam state remains a derived presentation/lock contract over Drive, not a second resource.
8. The HUD shows one weapon resource bar and no component-composition readout.

The balance implication is deliberately neutral: removing the retired composition path also removes
its special damage and cost modifiers. Authored dual weapons keep their existing definitions and
combos; no weapon number changes in B27.
