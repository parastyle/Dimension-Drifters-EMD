# Weapon bank — post-B27 systems rules

Status: current. This replaces the 2026-07-18 composition proposal.

- A player owns individual weapon instances.
- Each Stash row, Active position, and Pack position contains one instance.
- The three Active positions are independent; selecting one equips only that weapon.
- Same-class weapons may occupy different positions without creating a relationship.
- Pre-made duals are one authored weapon instance: they use one position, render their own two
  parts, and execute their authored combo.
- Carry is committed before launch. Carried and found instances remain at risk under the existing
  extraction/defeat rules.
- Rarity, affix, provenance, tier, exact identity, curator weighting, sale value, Intake overflow,
  shelf capacity, and prestige consequences remain unchanged.
- The shared Drive resource remains global. A position switch never refills it or transfers
  per-weapon cooldown debt.

The player-facing armory has no composition type filter or combine/split action. Its detail and
capacity copy describe individual instances and cells.
